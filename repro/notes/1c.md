# 1c: Author's distilled model and variance

## Import and probe
- pacman-0.8b-qwen imported from Hugging Face (Q8_0, 833 MB) with `CAPABILITY decision` appended to the Modelfile.
- Runs on the `llamacpp` runner, 917 MB in memory (tev1:0.8b runs on `mlx` at 10 GB). This model's runner likely matches the author's setup.
- Probe: accuracy 0.88, frightened 13/13, p50 447 ms (author in-game: 55 ms on M3 Ultra). Compare tev1:0.8b 0.35, tev1:4b 0.82.

## Lockstep (seeds 100-109)
- Mean 379.9 pellets (author: 417). Per game: 615, 239, 472, 239, 476, 438, 225, 231, 480, 384.
- Bimodal: 4 games end before clearing level 1 (225-239), 6 clear it and reach level 2-3 (384-615). The mean depends mostly on how many games clear level 1.
- sd ~138, SE of the 10-game mean ~44. The gap to the author (37) is under one SE: consistent with the author's result.
- Lockstep is deterministic, so an identical environment would give identical games. The gap means the environment differs (Ollama 0.40.3 vs 0.35.x, llama.cpp numerics): a tiny numeric difference can flip a near-tie decision and the whole game diverges. Per-game spread is the right yardstick for how much that can move the mean.
- Per-game p50 530-672 ms in lockstep on this Air.
- Author's notes (model_evaluation.md line 492): on Ollama 0.35.1 the published model reproduced exactly 417 lockstep and 61.9% agreement. Lockstep is bit-for-bit reproducible within one Ollama version. Author's means: score 7,649, 86 s survival; mine: 5,903, 76 s.
- Hypothesis: the Ollama version (0.40.3 vs 0.35.1) alone explains 379.9 vs 417. Test tracked in an issue: rerun on 0.35.1, expect exactly 417.

## Realtime run-to-run variance (3 rounds, alternating with tev1:0.8b)
- pacman-0.8b-qwen: 206.7, 204.9, 206.5 (mean 206.0, run-to-run sd ~1). Author on M3 Ultra: 441, 467, 366 (sd ~52).
- tev1:0.8b (control): 43.6, 36.1, 34.7 (mean 38.1, sd ~4.7). No shared drift between the two models: machine conditions were stable.
- Why so stable: at ~0.53 s per decision and ~26% stale, no game in 30 cleared level 1 (all 189-230). Lockstep is bimodal (stuck at ~230 vs clearing to 380-615); latency removes the upper mode, and with it most of the variance. Variance depends on the regime, not just the model.
- Latency cost: same model, lockstep 379.9 vs realtime 206.0 here vs realtime 425 on the author's machine (55 ms).
- Implication for Stage 2: use lockstep as the primary metric (measures judgment), with more seeds and paired comparisons. Per-game sd ~138, so 10 seeds give SE ~44; 50 seeds give ~20, and pairing by seed reduces it further. Realtime on this Air has tiny variance but mostly measures latency.
