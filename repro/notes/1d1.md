# 1d-1: Oracle-label student trained on MLX

## Data
- gen-data with oracle-5s labels, author's players and seeds: 57,249 train states (author 57,231), 8,050 val (author 8,047).
- build_sft --target values --temperature 50: 31,585 train rows (author 31,585, exact), 4,975 val rows.
- Dedup kept 89% of oracle-5s states, 85% of random, 26% of greedy (greedy revisits identical states).
- 49% of train rows have a target max > 0.9 (decisive); 16% are near-ties.

## Training (train_mlx.py, 1 epoch, micro-batch 4, no grad checkpointing)
- 988 steps; ~33-35 s/step when the Mac was idle (author M3 Ultra: 4.2 s/step); peak memory 13.7 GB.
- Validation (first 2000 val rows, bf16, agreement with the oracle's best option):

| step | acc | ce |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0.296 | 1.423 |
| 200 | 0.395 | 1.157 |
| 400 | 0.461 | 1.119 |
| 600 | 0.497 | 1.100 |
| 800 | 0.513 | 1.085 |
| 988 | 0.521 | 1.082 |

- Diminishing returns, no overfitting (val ce decreases monotonically), still slowly improving at the end (unlike the author's teacher-label run, which plateaued after step 600). A second epoch may add 1-2 points.
- Pre-training baseline (acc 0.296, ce 1.423) is the alignment target for my own trainer in 1d-3.
- Lesson: closing the lid pauses training; it resumes on wake. --resume is only for a dead process. Check `pgrep -fl train_mlx` first.

## Export
- export_gguf_mlx.sh: merged bf16 to runs/ft_v1_mlx/merged, Q8_0 GGUF 795 MB (same size as the author's).
- Quantization printed RuntimeWarnings (overflow in divide, invalid value in subtract/cast) in gguf-py quants.py. Likely blocks of near-zero weights whose scale d underflows, so 1/d overflows; their dequantized values (int x d) stay ~0. To be confirmed by the bf16 vs Q8_0 parity check.

## Import and parity (bf16 training weights vs served Q8_0, 50 val states)
- Imported as pacman-v1-mlx via /api/create with SYSTEM from training/prompt.py and the decision capability.
- Same choice on 48/50; max |prob difference| on any option 0.033 (author: 49/50 for the MLX student).
- Both disagreements (seed 940, ticks 296 and 1258) have a training top-2 gap of exactly 0.0: up and back tie, and the two sides break ties differently. Effectively 50/50. The quantization RuntimeWarnings had no visible effect.
- Script: repro/scripts/parity.py (reuse in 1d-2 and 1d-3).

## Evaluation
- Agreement with the oracle (1000 val states, served Q8_0): 0.527, acc_decisive 0.538, ce 1.088. Author's oracle-label student (pacman-0.8b, 2 epochs): 0.539. Author's published model on my val set: 0.544. Within one SE.
- Lockstep (seeds 100-109): 367.3 pellets. Per game: 431, 234, 235, 423, 427, 349, 404, 470, 350, 350. Author's published model on the same seeds and machine: 379.9.
- Paired by seed: mean diff -12.6, sd ~163, SE ~52, t ~ -0.24. Not distinguishable. 1d-1 reproduced: a free oracle-label student trained on a MacBook Air matches the published model.
- Pairing barely helps: per-seed results of the two models are almost uncorrelated, because one different choice sends the game down a different path. Stage 2 needs more seeds, not just pairing.
- Style differs: mine clears level 1 in 8/10 games (author's model 6/10), eats more ghosts (8.8 vs 6.3 per game), and scores more points (7,069 vs 5,903) with slightly fewer pellets. Likely objective mismatch: the oracle maximizes points over 5 s (ghosts are worth 200-1600, pellets 10), while the ladder metric is pellets.
