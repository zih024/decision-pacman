# 1a: Environment + determinism

## Observations from watching tev1:0.8b and playing myself

- The model reliably avoids ghosts.
- It does not seem to use power pellets strategically. It does not go for a power pellet to make the ghosts slow down (frightened mode), and it does not chase frightened ghosts to send them back to the ghost house.
- When there are no pellets near Pac-Man, the model seems lost and does not know which way to go.
- When I play, I can see that the 2-3 tiles around me in every direction are empty, but that there are pellets farther away, so I head toward them. The model does not seem to have that sense of where the remaining pellets are.
- Update: random at early commit 4804ad2 also gives 27.6 with identical per-game results. The code-change hypothesis is wrong. Random is reproducible on the author's code; the 30.3 in the author's notes could not be traced. Not pursued further (weakest baseline, gap < 3 pellets, greedy matches).

## Determinism test (lockstep, seeds 100-109)

- greedy: two runs identical per game. Lockstep mean 354.1 pellets vs realtime 200.7, with far fewer decisions (581 vs 1705 on seed 100). The clocks differ in when decisions are asked and applied, not only in latency. TODO: explain from scripts/run_headless.ts.
- tev1:0.8b: two runs identical, and all 10 seeds identical (1 pellet, 1048 decisions, 35 s). Not a pipeline bug: the model picks "back" at every query and oscillates between junctions 12,23 and 15,23, one tick apart. "back" always shows the most pellets (10), so reversing is always preferred. Pac-Man never eats a power pellet, ghosts are deterministic outside frightened mode, so the seed has no effect.
- p50 8 ms is Ollama cache hits on the two repeating states. Fresh-state latency from the probe: p50 285 ms on this MacBook Air (author: 62 ms on M3 Ultra in-game).
- Implication: lockstep removes the latency noise that lets tev1:0.8b escape this loop in realtime. The author reports tev1:0.8b only in realtime (69).
- Implication for evaluation: seeds only matter when frightened-mode randomness is triggered. For policies that never eat power pellets, 10 seeds may be one game repeated 10 times.
- Confirmed on seed 100: 1047 of 1048 choices were "back"; junctions 12,23 (525) and 15,23 (507), one escape to 18,23 (16). I also saw this back-and-forth occasionally in the browser (realtime), where it sometimes ends in death. Realtime latency sometimes breaks the loop; lockstep never does.
